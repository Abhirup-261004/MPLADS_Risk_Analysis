Composite MP & Constituency Risk Scorecard

1. Objective of Feature 7
In the MPLADS Risk Analytics framework, individual pipeline features generate specialized anomaly signals:

Feature 1 detects fund disbursement shortfalls and completion integrity breaches.
Feature 2 detects sanction-stage cost overruns and pricing anomalies relative to state peer groups.
Feature 5 detects vendor spend concentration, single-MP contractor monopolies, and collusion risks.
While each feature provides high-value localized metrics, requiring oversight authorities (such as the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation - MoSPI, State Nodal Authorities, or District Magistrates) to evaluate separate, un-unified data feeds creates operational friction and decision fatigue.

The primary objective of Feature 7 is to serve as the Unified Composite Risk Scorecard Layer. It synthesizes the three domain sub-scores into a single, mathematically sound, explainable, and ranked portfolio-level risk metric for every Member of Parliament (
).

Core Goals:
Eliminate Scale Dominance & Methodological Bias: Replace arbitrary unstandardized formulas with population-normalized Z-scores and empirical correlation diagnostics.
Integrate Multi-Dimensional Anomaly Detection: Combine rule-based statistical scorecards with unsupervised ML models (K-Means/GMM clustering and Isolation Forest).
Decouple Administrative Efficiency from Fraud Risk: Surface financial utilization (allocation_utilization_pct) as an independent context metric rather than blending it into corruption risk.
Deliver Explainable Dashboard Products: Generate natural-language audit explanations, priority audit worklists, and API-ready data structures for immediate decision support.
